# SuretyOS — Landing Page Build Spec

**Version 1.0 · Waitlist / Founding-Agency Launch**

---

## 1. Objective

A single-page site with one job: **capture qualified surety agencies as founding members before the product is built.**

This is not a feature site. It is a demand-validation and lead-capture tool. Success is measured by qualified signups (agencies that write real bond volume) and the conversations they unlock — not by raw traffic or vanity signups.

**Primary CTA:** Join as a founding agency (email capture + qualification fields).
**Secondary goal:** Communicate the product vision clearly enough that an agency owner immediately recognizes their own pain.

---

## 2. Target Visitor

- **Who:** Owners, principals, and producers at US surety bond agencies (5–25 staff).
- **Mindset on arrival:** Skeptical, busy, has seen generic insurance software before. Needs to feel *understood* in the first 5 seconds.
- **What converts them:** Recognition of their exact daily pain (retyping bond data, chasing renewals), plus a credible founder who clearly knows surety — especially court bonds.

**Design implication:** Lead with the problem in their language. No jargon-free fluff, no generic "streamline your workflow" SaaS speak. Specific beats clever.

## 5. The Signup Form (most important element)

The form fields double as your qualification survey. Keep it short but capture the qualifying data.

**Fields:**
| Field | Type | Required | Purpose |
|-------|------|----------|---------|
| Full name | text | yes | Personalization |
| Work email | email | yes | Contact + signal (work domain = real) |
| Agency name | text | yes | Qualification |
| Bond types written | multi-select | yes | Segment them (Court / Commercial / Construction / Fidelity / Mixed) |
| Monthly bond volume | single-select | yes | Size them (1–25 / 26–100 / 100+ / Not sure) |
| Anything painful about your current workflow? | textarea | no | Gold for messaging + call prep |

**Submit button:** `Claim My Founding Spot`

**On submit:**
- Show a success state: *"You're in. Check your email — we'll be in touch within 48 hours to set up a quick call."*
- Trigger confirmation email (see Section 7).

**Do NOT** ask for phone number on the form — it depresses conversion. Get it on the call.

## 6. Design Direction

- **Brand palette:** Navy `#1A3A5C` (primary), Teal `#0E7C86` (accent/CTA), Mint `#02C39A` (highlights), light grey `#EEF3F8` (section backgrounds), white. Dark text `#1F1F2E`.
- **Tone:** Confident, plain-spoken, industry-credible. No startup buzzwords. Talk like someone who knows surety.
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, or similar). Large readable headings, generous spacing.
- **Layout:** Single column, mobile-first. Most agency owners will open this on a phone from a LinkedIn link.
- **CTAs:** Teal buttons, high contrast, repeated 3x down the page. Always the same action.
- **Imagery:** Product mockups if available; clean abstract/workflow graphics if not. Avoid generic stock photos of people shaking hands.
- **Trust signals:** "SOC 2-aligned," "Founder-built," founding-spots counter.

## 7. Post-Signup Flow

1. **Instant confirmation email** (via Resend, matching your stack):
   - Subject: *"You're a SuretyOS founding agency — here's what's next"*
   - Body: thank them, set expectation (call within 48 hrs), one line reinforcing the founder-pricing benefit.
2. **Internal notification** to you (email or Slack) with the new signup's details so you can follow up fast.
3. **Manual follow-up within 48 hours** — personal note asking for a 15-minute call. This is where waitlist becomes pipeline.

## 8. Technical Requirements

- **Stack:** Keep it simple. Next.js or plain React + Vite, or even a static site (Astro/HTML). This is one page — don't over-engineer.
- **Hosting:** Vercel or Netlify (free tier, instant deploy, custom domain).
- **Form handling:** Store submissions somewhere you can act on them:
  - Simplest: a form service (Formspree, Tally, or a Google Sheet via API).
  - Better (matches your stack): a small API endpoint writing to a database table, since you'll reuse this infra later.
- **Email:** Resend for the confirmation email (you're already using it in the product).
- **Analytics:** Plausible or Google Analytics — track visits, scroll depth, and form conversion rate.
- **Domain:** A clean domain — suretyos.com or get-suretyos.com. Use the real product domain so the waitlist URL builds brand equity.
- **Performance:** Fast load, mobile-optimized, no heavy frameworks. Lighthouse score 90+.

## 9. Explicitly Out of Scope (for v1)

Keep the first version lean. Do **not** build:
- Login / accounts / any app functionality
- Live product demo or interactive sandbox
- Pricing page with tiers (pricing is "founder pricing, shared on call")
- Blog / resources / content hub
- Multiple pages — this is one page only
- Live chat widget

Ship the one page, drive traffic, capture qualified agencies, book calls. Everything else is a distraction at this stage.
